## 📚 Acknowledgments & Scientific Datasets

The antimicrobial resistance (AMR) gene signatures, resistance profiles, and phenotype catalogs embedded in this project are curated from public scientific databases:

1. **NCBI (National Center for Biotechnology Information):** Reference bacterial genomes and curated AMR gene sequences.
2. **CARD (The Comprehensive Antibiotic Resistance Database):** Curated resistance determinant sequences, ontologies, and mechanism classifications.
3. **ResFinder (Center for Genomic Epidemiology):** Acquired antimicrobial resistance gene database and phenotype mappings.
